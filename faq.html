FAQ

What kind of organisations do you support?

We work with universities, public organisations, healthcare teams, and innovation projects that need practical support in AI, privacy, and funding-related delivery.

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

Normally within 1-2 business days. For urgent requests, mention urgency in your subject line.

Do you offer one-off reviews and long-term support?

Yes. We support both short diagnostics and ongoing advisory roles, depending on project maturity and team needs.
