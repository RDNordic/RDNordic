Identity & Privacy

Biometrics in the Age of AI

A forthcoming field note from the RD-Nordic-Identity workstream on biometric identification, large-scale sensitive data collection, and the consequences of weak guardrails.

Andrew Davidson
R&D Nordic · 27 June 2026 · 2 min read

This forthcoming article grows out of the RD-Nordic-Identity workstream. It looks at what changes when biometric identification moves from a narrow verification tool into wider AI-enabled infrastructure: the mass gathering of sensitive data, the pressure to normalise face and identity checks, and the real-world consequences when legal, organisational, and technical guardrails are not strong enough.

The first case study will focus on Persona, not as a broad accusation against one company, but because it sits close to the centre of a fast-moving identity verification market. The question is whether early AI-era identity companies, their customers, and the public institutions around them fully understand the scale of responsibility that comes with biometric systems.

Forthcoming

The full article is being developed from current research and will be published here once the case study and supporting material are ready for public release.
