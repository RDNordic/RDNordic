04 / About

Hello, I’m Andrew

I’m Andrew Davidson, a multidisciplinary R&D adviser based in Hamar, Norway. My background brings together behavioural science, research, AI, privacy and ethics. I connect the technical questions with the people, organisations and rules around them.

Through R&D Nordic, I work with universities, public bodies, businesses and research partners on project development, responsible AI and practical governance. The work might be a focused conversation, a workshop, a prototype or a funding application.

Alongside the company, I work as an R&D and privacy adviser in Norwegian higher education. The roles are separate. In both settings, I care about leaving people with something clearer, stronger and more likely to work.
